Privacy Policy

How Modjadji Projects collects, uses and protects information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 27/09/2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains what information Modjadji Projects collects through this website, why we collect it, and how it is handled, in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to submit through the forms on this site:

  • Contact / quote request form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you're interested in, and a description of your project.
  • Client review form: your name, what best describes you (e.g. homeowner, business owner), your location (optional), a star rating, and your written review.

We do not use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics tools to monitor visitors on this site beyond what is described in Section 5 below.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your enquiry and provide a quote.
  • Reviews are used to display client feedback on this website, once approved by us. Reviews are not published automatically we moderate every submission before it appears publicly.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How Your Information Is Stored

Contact form submissions are sent directly to our business email address and are not stored in a database by this website.

5. Third-Party Services

This website relies on the following third-party services to operate. Each processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Maps — an embedded map showing our workshop location on the Contact section of this site. Google may set cookies related to this embed under its own privacy policy.
  • Email delivery service (Gmail/Google Workspace) — used to receive contact form submissions.

6. Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. The embedded Google Maps widget on our Contact page may set cookies as part of Google's own services see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

7. Data Retention

We retain enquiry emails for 1 months" and "Rejected or removed reviews are permanently deleted after 12 months.

8. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request that it be corrected or deleted, and object to how it is processed. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10 below.

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the way this website handles information changes. The version published here will always reflect our current practice.

12. Contact Us

For any questions about this policy, or to exercise your rights under POPIA, please reach us through our contact form, or the phone number and email address listed on our homepage.
