Who We Are

A Family Tradition of Fine Woodworking

Modjadji Projects was founded by Lehlogonolo Modjadji,A young carpenter turned his love for woodwork into a small business, crafting sturdy and stylish furniture. With skill, creativity, and determination, he quickly built a reputation in his community, proving that passion can grow into success.

We believe every piece of wood has a story — and our job is to reveal it. Whether you're dreaming of custom built-in shelving, a handcrafted dining table, or a complete home renovation, we approach every project with the same meticulous care and attention to detail.
