How It Works

From your first message to the finished piece in your home — here's what to expect when you work with us.

01

Tell Us What You Need

Submit your project requirements through our contact form — dimensions, materials, timeline and budget all help us understand what you're looking for.

02

Discuss Your Project

We'll get in touch to discuss measurements, materials, design and any specific requirements — including a site visit where needed.

03

Get Your Quote

You'll receive a quotation based on your project's scope, materials and finishes, so you know exactly what to expect before any work begins.

04

We Build

Once you've approved the quote, your project is crafted in our workshop according to the agreed specifications.

05

Installation

Where applicable, we deliver and install the completed work at your property, so it's ready to use from day one.
