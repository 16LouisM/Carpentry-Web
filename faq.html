What areas do you service?

We're based in Soshanguve and serve Benoni, Hammanskraal, Ga-Rankuwa, Mabopane, and surrounding areas within a 30-kilometer radius. If you're just outside that range, get in touch — we may still be able to help.

What services do you offer?

Custom furniture, kitchen and cabinetry work, decking and outdoor structures, home renovations (doors, skirting, staircases, flooring), repairs and restoration of existing woodwork, and design consultation if you're not yet sure what you need.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the project request form on our homepage with details about what you're looking for — dimensions, materials, timeline and budget all help us give you a more accurate estimate. We aim to respond within 24 hours.
